Token Vortex - Privacy Policy

Effective date: 6 November 2026

This policy covers the Android game Token Vortex (package name com.noitues.tokenvortex), published on Google Play by Noitues ("we", "us").

Summary

Token Vortex does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. The game has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no in-app purchases and no third-party software development kits. It does not request the internet permission and cannot send data off your device.

Data stored on your device

The game saves your progress and your settings (for example sound volume and text size) in a file in the app's private storage on your device. This file never leaves your device. It is not included in Android cloud backups.

Permissions

The game requests one permission: Vibrate, used for haptic feedback during the game. You can turn haptics off in the game's settings menu.

Deleting your data

You can delete the saved game at any time from the in-game menu (Menu, then Erase save). Uninstalling the game removes all of its data from your device. Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to delete on request.

Information from Google Play

Google Play, which distributes the game, may collect information such as installs and, if you have chosen to share usage and diagnostics data with Google, crash reports. Google provides us with this information only in aggregated or anonymised form; we cannot use it to identify you. Google's handling of that information is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

Token Vortex is not directed at children under 13. The game does not collect personal data from any player of any age.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game changes how data is handled, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.
